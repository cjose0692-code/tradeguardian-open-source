# Mission and principles

## The problem

Traders can write clear rules and still abandon them under pressure. Position sizing, trading hours, trade frequency, and risk limits need to be connected to the decisions made during a session.

## Our mission

Help traders turn personal trading plans into repeatable habits through clear rules, deliberate decision checkpoints, structured review, and visible accountability.

## Principles

1. **Rules before emotion.** Set boundaries before the session and make them understandable at the moment of action.
2. **User-defined discipline.** Support the trader's own plan rather than presenting one strategy as suitable for everyone.
3. **Process over promises.** Evaluate rule adherence separately from profit and loss. A profitable trade can still violate a rule.
4. **Transparent limits.** Describe what a workflow can observe and enforce. Missing data must not be treated as proof of compliance.
5. **Privacy by design.** Keep proprietary implementation, credentials, and individual trading data out of public documentation.
6. **Learning through review.** Use journals and decision records to identify recurring behavior and inform changes to a plan.

## Intended impact

The goal is to reduce avoidable departures from a trader's own plan and make behavior easier to review. This is an intended outcome, not a claim of measured effectiveness. This repository publishes no verified user, revenue, performance, or loss-reduction statistics.
